# Cranium preview source

The app icon derives from a Cranium preview rendered from Human Atlas Rust's
Z-Anatomy GLB package with its WASM renderer. Source geometry and materials were
preserved; the camera angle, lighting and transparent background were chosen
for the app's Home page. The original PNG is not bundled with this site.

| Image | Source selection | Source model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| anatomy.png | Cranium (collection:75ab39af73cd3c0a), 49 structures | skeletal-system.glb |

The Cranium preview omits the lateral processes of nasal septal cartilage,
major alar cartilages and nasal septal cartilage.

Credit: Gauthier Kervyn and the Z-Anatomy contributors, based on BodyParts3D by
the Database Center for Life Science. The adapted model packages retain the
upstream CC BY-SA 4.0 license and the component-specific notices.
The source-derived preview and icon retain those terms.
