# Home model previews

These PNG previews were rendered from the app's Z-Anatomy GLB packages with its
existing WASM renderer. Source geometry and materials were preserved; the camera
angle, lighting and transparent background were chosen for the Home page.

| Image | Source selection | Source model |
| --- | --- | --- |
| anatomy.png | Cranium (collection:75ab39af73cd3c0a), 49 structures | skeletal-system.glb |
| lessons.png | Brain (collection:8ef3d641cd88cc7d), 250 structures | nervous-system.glb |
| games.png | Heart (collection:2cf0b96099745444), 17 structures | cardiovascular-system.glb |

The Cranium preview omits the lateral processes of nasal septal cartilage,
major alar cartilages and nasal septal cartilage.
